DEPLOYMENT CHECKLIST:
================================

STEP 1: UPDATE YOUR DOMAIN
When deploying, replace every development or placeholder URL with the final domain in:
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- index.html metadata
- index.html JSON-LD schema

STEP 2: GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE
1. Go to Google Search Console (search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add your website property
3. Submit sitemap.xml
4. Verify ownership

STEP 3: GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
1. Create/Claim profile at google.com/business
2. Add:
   - Business photos (5-10)
   - Operating hours
   - Phone number
   - Address
   - Services offered
3. Encourage reviews (customers can review)

STEP 4: BING WEBMASTER TOOLS
1. Go to bing.com/webmasters
2. Submit your site
3. Upload sitemap.xml

STEP 5: ANALYTICS & TRACKING
1. Google Analytics 4 - Add tracking code to <head>
2. Google Tag Manager - For conversion tracking
3. Hotjar - For user behavior
